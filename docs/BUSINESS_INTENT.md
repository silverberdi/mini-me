# Business Intent

## Why mini me exists
Manual use of multiple coding agents creates coordination work: selecting capacity, moving context, controlling scope, tracking retries and quota limits, reviewing changes, validating UI, preparing PRs, deploying candidates and remembering what remains safe to close.

mini me should turn that coordination burden into a small **human decision inbox**.

## Job to be done
> Take implementation-ready OpenSpec changes across my projects, execute them with available subscription capacity, independently review and audit the result, preserve work through failures and quota exhaustion, provide a testable UI candidate when human validation is required, and bring me only decisions that require human judgment.

## Product posture
- Personal internal system; not a commercial product.
- One controlled Linux installation.
- Multi-project by domain model from day one.
- Optimize for reliability, traceability, low unnecessary spend and reduced human coordination.
- Do not build multitenancy, generic installer ecosystems, arbitrary deployment backends or enterprise portability unless a later real need appears.

## Success
mini me is successful when a well-defined change can move from READY to the appropriate human gate without manual agent handoffs, while preserving exact evidence of what was built, reviewed, audited and—when UI applies—what candidate the human actually tested.
