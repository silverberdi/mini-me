# Scope and Roadmap Guard
Compare intended edits with active proposal/specs/tasks and `docs/ROADMAP.md`. Reject opportunistic future-stage features, dependency upgrades unrelated to the change, broad refactors and speculative abstractions. Difficulty is not a reason to abandon or reassign scope.
